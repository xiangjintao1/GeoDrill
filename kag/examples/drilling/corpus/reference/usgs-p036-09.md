Rotational speed of the drill should not exceed 200 r/min; if chatter or tearing out the gravels is indicated, the drill shoukl be slowed until the drill pipe is running smoothly. Using the low down pressure on the core bit and relatively low rotational speeds for axing this type of material results in a slow penetration rate (in the range of 1 ft every 15-20 min), because, even though the drill is coring in an unconsolidated formation, it is also coring rock (the gravel particles).
