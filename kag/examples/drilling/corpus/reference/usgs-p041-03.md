# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页41 第3段

Starting or seating in of the core barrel is accomplished using minimal down pressure and turning slowly. After the core barrel has been started, the down pressure and rotational speed are increased for obtaining optimum penetration rate in the formation being drilled. Down pressure and the rotational speed are variable, depending on the hardness of the rock and the diameter of the core
