Three general types of drilling fluids are: (1) water with either native clays or commercial, highyield bentonites added; (2) mud-laden, oil-base mixtures; and (3) air. Mud-laden, oil-base muds have no applications in drilling for ground-water investigations; and air, as a drilling fluid, is discussed on page 29.
