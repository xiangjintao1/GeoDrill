# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页37 第3段

Because of the ease of forming a filter cake on low-permeability material such as clays, a lower viscosity drilling mud can be used (35-s to 40-s); with clay, use of the thinnest drilling mud possible to clean and lubricate the bit allows faster penetration rates. The coring bit may be rotated as high as 400-500 r/min when coring thick clay beds if the drill pipe rotates smoothly. This higher rotational speed, used in conjunction with a compatible downfeed pressure on the coring bit, also aids in faster coring penetration rates when coring clay.
