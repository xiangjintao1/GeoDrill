This inner-tube adjustment is made by loosening the holding nut on the inner-barrel spindle assembly (threaded rod) and turning the threaded spindle rod into or out of the swivel assembly. The amount of adjustment is made by trial and error; the adjustment, reassembly, disassembly, and adjustment may have to be done several times to accomplish the proper clearance (about l/16 of one inch) to avoid slack in the bearings, particularly after use. To check the inner tube for proper clearance, the assembled barrel is placed horizontally and the inner tube is pushed up. The core will force the inner tube up to this position when coring is being done.
