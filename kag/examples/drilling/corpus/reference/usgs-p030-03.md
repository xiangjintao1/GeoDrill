Some of the basic techniques involved in hydraulicrotary coring parallels the techniques of hydraulicrotary drilling. A drilling fluid is circulated to carry cuttings out of the hole, cool the bit, lubricate the rotating drill pipe, and so forth. Some major components used in hydraulic-rotary coring, such as drilling rigs and mud pumps, may be identical to those used for hydraulic-rotary drilling.
