When the overshot assembly reaches the inner-barrel head assembly, the overshot latching assembly engages the inner-barrel spearhead, and the inner-barrel assembly is hoisted to the surface. After the innerbarrel assembly has been removed from the drill rod, another complete inner-barrel assembly is dropped down the drill rod and allowed to settle through the drilling mud or is slowly pumped down to bottom and coring can be resumed. This removal procedure is followed for each core interval.
