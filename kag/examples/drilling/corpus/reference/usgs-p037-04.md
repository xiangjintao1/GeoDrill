# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页37 第4段

Although use of a thinner drilling mud and a higher rotational speed of the coring bit accomplishes fairly fast penetration rate, one phenomenon occurs in coring clay to impose a restriction on the penetration rate: when the diamond coring bit cuts through a clay, particularly if the clay is somewhat dense, a very close tolerance hole is cut because little or no material erodes. This results in the need for high pump pressures to push the cuttings through this tight restriction; and, even though a thin drilling mud is used, a fluid pressure as high as 100 lb/in.’ might be needed to accomplish this.
