# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页45 第2段

After the core barrel is removed from the hole and placed in a pipe vise or on a clean platform, the diamond bit is removed using a pipe wrench or chain wrench, and extreme care is taken not to affm the wrench to the bit or reaming shell opposite any diamonds. Then the outer tube is broken loose and removed from the core-barrel head assembly exposing the inner tube. The inner tube is disconnected from the inner part of the core-barrel head assembly and the core-retainer shoe removed. Then the inner tube is slightly inclined to allow the core to slide into the segmented compartments of the core boxes. The core is marked with the depth, top and bottom of the core, and any other pertinent information.
