The representativeness of a given sample is a function of variability in the sample in relation to the scale of variability in the formation from which the sample is taken. For example, a completely homogeneous, isotropic material requires only one sample, infinitely variable material requires an infinite number of samples. Statistical approaches are available for attacking this problem, but, because of its complexity, the problem is considered to be beyond the scope of this discussion.
