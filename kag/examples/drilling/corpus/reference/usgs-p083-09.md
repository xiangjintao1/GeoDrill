# USGS手册 岩心质量 样品扰动 代表性 含水率 保存 运输 印刷页83 第9段

Where economics or other considerations dictate, disturbed samples may be used for determinations of particle-size distribution, specific gravity, and lithologic or mineralogic analysis. In using disturbed materials, the following considerations should be kept in mind. (1) Material being returned to the surface by a power auger can rarely be relied upon to represent actual particle-size distribution in the sediments peneterated, especially when auger drilling below the water table. Loose, granular materials encountered in the hole will probably contribute excessively to the sample appearing at the surface; fine-grained materials may not be recovered.
