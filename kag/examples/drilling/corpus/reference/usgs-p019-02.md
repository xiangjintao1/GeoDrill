# USGS手册 冲洗液 泥浆 黏度 密度 含砂量 泥皮 孔壁稳定 抽吸 印刷页19 第2段

Drilling mud serves to: (1) remove the cuttings generated by the cutting action of the bit from the bottom of the hole; (2) cool the bit and lubricate the bit bearings if the bit is the roller-cone type; (3) build a filter cake on the hole wall preventing fluid loss in mud invasion of penetrated formations; (4) support and prevent caving of the wall of the hole; (5) control the formation pressures of water or gas preventing these fluids from coming into the hole; and (6) lubricate the string of drill pipe as it rotates in the hole. These purposes are not listed in any order of importance.
