# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页49 第3段

If geoscientists wish to obtain a core sample from a formation for measuring moisture content, they would probably specify air-rotary coring rather than mud-rotary coring, because mud-rotary coring would contaminate the sample and air probably would not. They are partly right: air might not contaminate the sample with foreign materials; however, it will possibly dry or blow a percentage of the moisture out of the sample. An experiment conducted by the research-drilling project provides an example of air-drying of moisture in a core.
