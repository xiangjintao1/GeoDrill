5. Design of a drilling-mud program for hydraulic-rotary coring of rock is for the benefit of the hydrologist (or other scientist) requiring core. If the cores are to be used for determinations of any chemical or other waste materials that may be contained in the pore spaces of the rock, water or thin drilling fluid cannot be used because of the danger of flushing out the constituents of interest. This problem is more pronounced as permeability of the rock increases; a designed drilling-mud program may need to be written into the drillingcontract specifications to build a filter cake on the exterior of the core as well as on the interior of the borehole.
