2. Some drillers use the drilling-in method of installing casing, particularly if the overburden is fine-grained material. A diamond cutting bit on the bottom section of drill casing, and circulating drilling fluid is used to drill the casing through the overburden and down to consolidated material. If this method is used, a chopping bit may be needed to clean out accumulated debris from inside the drill casing. This drilling-in of the casing is preferred when alternate hard and soft formations are anticipated.
