# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页48 第6段

The Water Resources Division of the U.S. Geological Survey sometimes contracts for air-rotary coring in possibly contaminated or known contaminated lithologic environments. Special coring procedures are used, such as grouted insurface casing; using drive-core or other samplers for obtaining cores of unconsolidated interbcddcd sediments; and using only wire-line-coring methods, to prevent smearing or downward contamination of the hole that could occur if conventional coring (tripping in and out of the hole) methods were used. Par discussions of these precautionary methods, see “Hydrology of the Solid-Waste Burial Ground, as Related to the Potential Migration of Radionuelides,” (Burgus and Maestas, 1975).
