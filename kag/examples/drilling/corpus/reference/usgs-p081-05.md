# USGS手册 岩心质量 样品扰动 代表性 含水率 保存 运输 印刷页81 第5段

Drive sampling usually causes greater compaction of samples than other methods of sampling, particularly in granular, noncohesive sediments. The amount of compaction may be directly related to the size of the core. Data suggest that the amount of compaction can probably be closely correlated to the number of hammer impacts and the total energy absorbed by the sample in the driving process. Although no quantitative data are available on this point, we suspecte that any drive sample requiring more than 20 or 25 blows per ft, using a 30-in. drop with a 140-lb hammer, will be significantly disturbed, probably throughout its entire volume.
