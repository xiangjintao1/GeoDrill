100 ft, a medium sand with a fairly high permeability is encountered. Building a quick filter cake on the core as well as the hole wall is imperative, to prevent invasion of the core and fluid loss to the formation. We have previously suggested a lightweight, 75-s viscosity drilling mud for coring under these lithologic conditions. A greater pump pressure is required to move this higher viscosity drilling mud through the bit opening and up the annulus formed between the drill pipe and the borehole. Where 35-50-lb/in.* pressure was used for the 50-s viscosity drilling mud, 50-75-lb/in.2 pressure is used as coring progresses and cuttings are generated.
