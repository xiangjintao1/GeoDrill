# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页48 第2段

Much of the equipment used for air-rotary coring is basically the same type of equipment as that used for hydraulic-rotary coring. Drill pipe used for coring by both methods are interchangeable and wire-line coring or conventional coring can be accomplished using either air-rotary or hydraulicrotary drilling method. Air-rotary coring bits and core barrels are constructed with discharge ports and air passageways larger than those used for hydraulicrotary coring; otherwise, they are quite similarly constructed.
