# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页36 第7段

A gravel with a sand-silt-clay matrix is considered by many people to be impossible to core without causing considerable disturbance. This type of material is commonly found in glacial till and sometimes occurs in alluvium. It is usually considerably less permeable than sands, so a lessviscous drilling mud can be used (possibly in the 40-s to 50-s range) to core it. This thinner mud will results in less fluid pressure required to circulate the cuttings to the surface; even at a depth of 300 ft, the pump pressure probably is less than that needed at a depth of 100 ft using a 75-s mud.
