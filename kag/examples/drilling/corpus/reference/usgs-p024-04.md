# USGS手册 冲洗液 泥浆 黏度 密度 含砂量 泥皮 孔壁稳定 抽吸 印刷页24 第4段

1. Remove the string of drill pipe from the hole at a relatively slow rate to minimize differences in hydrostatic head between the formation and the hole. Equalization of these pressure forces can only be accomplished if the inside of the drill string is vented to the atmosphere, allowing a completely unrestricted movement of fluid out of the pipe and bit so no changes in differential pressure occur between the fluid inside the hole and the formation. Pulling a string of drill pipe without a means for keeping the inside of the drill pipe open to the atmosphere (by using holes in the side of the pulling swivel or a regular water swivel) is referred to as dry pulling.
