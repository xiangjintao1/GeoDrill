Although forming a filter cake on clays is easy, and clays do not invade easily, a too-high fluid pressure eventually results in drilling mud invasion of the core; therefore, the penetration rate must be governed by the fluid-pressure buildup. If considerable thickness of clay is cored, drilling-mud viscosity increases, and the drilling mud may have to be thinned.
