# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页44 第5段

4. After the core has been logged, a 6-ft length of thin-wall, lay-flat, clear plastic tubing is placed over the core and the trough. Plastic tubing of this type can be purchased in various diameters and different-length rolls. One end of the tubing is then tied. A felt marking pen is used to mark the core as to top, bottom, depth, hole number, and any other necessary data. After the insleevcd core is marked, another half-round trough is placed over it. The core is then turned over and the trough that is still in the plastic sleeve is removed and the other end of the plastic sleeve is tied.
