# USGS手册 冲洗液 泥浆 黏度 密度 含砂量 泥皮 孔壁稳定 抽吸 印刷页19 第8段

The drill contractor expressed assurance that, from prior experience in drilling, the use of clear water as a drilling fluid would soon result in the formation of an adequate drilling mud composed of a mixture of the water and natural clays contained in the formation. Unfortunately, this did not occur; enough natural clay was never encountered in the upper part of the hole to form a lightweight, viscous mud. As a result, the native drilling fluid rapidly washed out the loosely consolidated sand zones, and total circulation of drilling fluid was lost. Circulation was not restored even though circulation-loss materials were used.
