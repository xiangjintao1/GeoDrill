# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页32 第9段

Hydraulic-rotary coring of unconsolidated sediments for the purpose of obtaining undisturbed cores is extremely difficult, compared to hydraulicrotary coring of rock. Most core drillers consider it impossible to core unconsolidated materials and, as a consequence, many will not attempt it. These unconsolidated materials are most commonly referred to as overburden and they are cased off in the exploration-drilling industry, prior to starting any wring operations. HoweveI; the overburden is usually of major interest in water-resource investigations.
