2. If borehole-geophysical logging or sampling methods are to be used that require no damage or bridging of the hole occur during removal of the string of drill pipe, the method of circulation during pulling should be used. This is a slow method, but it usually guarantees success in preventing damage to the drill hole upon removal of the string of drill pipe. Maintain circulation at a slow rate through the string of drill pipe at all times when it is being pulled. This guarantees that no great differential pressures will develop between the formation and drill hole; also, circulation will prevent buildup of muds and sands on the bit that make it act like a swab.
