If the hole is being cored into formations containing hydrostatic heads greater than that of the fluid column in the hole, then drilling-mud weight will have to be increased to overcome this hydrostatic-head difference. If added drilling-mud weight is required, it should be accomplished by adding barite and not by simply letting the sand content build to a high level. A high sand content is not only abrasive to the many drill components, but it also causes considerable eroding of the core.
