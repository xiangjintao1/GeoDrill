4. On-site storage should be for minimum lengths of time. If sampling is done over an extended period, groups of samples should be shipped at short intervals to the laboratory where they can be stored in a constant-temperature, constant-humidity room. Samples that are thrown into the back of a vehicle and carried about during a summer field season are generally of little value for laboratory testing.
