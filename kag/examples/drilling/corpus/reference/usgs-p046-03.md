# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页46 第3段

Partial or complete loss of drilling-fluid circulation sometimes occurs when hydraulic-rotary coring of rock is performed. This loss results from encountering fractures or high-intergranular permeability in the rock, large solution openings in limestone-associated rocks, and cavernous or scoriacinder zones in basalts (Teasdale, 1980). Blind coring is sometimes attempted (no drilling fluid circulated to the surface); however, this method could result in stuck or lost drill pipe.
