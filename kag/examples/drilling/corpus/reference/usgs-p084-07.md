3. Some commercially available samplers are effective in providing adequate sample recovery with minimum disturbance, if certain precautions are observed in their use. Drive samplers may be used with caution in granular, noncohesive media to avoid compaction of the material. Coring by rotary-drilling methods offers some advantages over drive sampling but introduces the possibility of contaminating the sample with drilling fluids. The design of certain rotary samplers minimizes the problem of contamination by allowing the cutting shoe to precede the rotary bit. Piston-drive samplers are excellent for recovery of soft or compressible sediments.
