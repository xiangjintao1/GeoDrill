A photograph of a rotary core of an unconsolidated medium sand obtained by the hydraulic-rotary coring method is shown in figure 19; the filter cake on the exterior of the core prevented or lessened mud invasion of the core. The amount of fluid to circulate for hydraulic-rotary coring is a variable that can only be discussed in general terms. Too little fluid will not permit proper cooling of the bit and lubrication of the tool string; it does not carry cuttings away from the face of the bit fast enough, resulting in bit blockage and high pump pressures.
