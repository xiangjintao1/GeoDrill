The wall effects referred to are those resulting from the confinement of samples in smooth-wall tubes for permeameter measurements. Laboratory experiments have demonstrated that apparent hydraulic conductivities may be significantly affected by the higher velocities of flow that occur along the walls of permeameter tubes. The effects can be correlated with medium-particle diameters in the samples.
