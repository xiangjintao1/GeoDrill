# USGS手册 冲洗液 泥浆 黏度 密度 含砂量 泥皮 孔壁稳定 抽吸 印刷页19 第3段

Many problems encountered in mud control result from a lack of understanding of the terms used in describing drilling muds. Par example, many people think of viscous mud as a weight property of the mud instead of a property of mud thickness (fig. 6). However, a viscous mud actually can be only slightly heavier than water; conversely, a heavy mud might be low in viscosity. These differences are important when considering desired purposes of the drilling mud in a drilling program. In general, use the lightest weight drilling mud possible considering the formation pressures and caving problems that may be encountered.
