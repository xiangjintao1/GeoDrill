# USGS手册 取心 岩心管 钻头 绳索 工艺 参数 冲洗液 印刷页29 第6段

The borehole is first flushed of all drilled cuttings. This can be accomplished by circulating air, air mist, foam, and (or) polymers in the hole until it is clean and no bridging or caving occurs. The drill pipe is then carefully removed from the borehole and the casing and screen are set in the open hole and sealed in place. After the installation is completed, the well is developed as necessary.
